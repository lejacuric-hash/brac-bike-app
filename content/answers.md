# Answer key for the app – Loda's Alphabet

The app compares what the player types against **Accepted answers**, after lower-casing and removing diacritics (č→c, ć→c, š→s, ž→z, đ→d or dj) and extra spaces. A correct answer reveals the stop's **Letter**, shown in Glagolitic, then decoded with Loda's key.

**The letter is fixed per stop and is the same in both languages.** The letters spell Croatian words from Nazor's sentence, so an English player answering "Chile" still gets Č. The player never has to extract a letter from the answer themselves.

"(with a note)" means: accept the answer, and show a short friendly note with the preferred form.

## Finale

- When all 20 letters are collected, the app shows the 12 unlocked words shuffled, and the player puts them in order.
- Correct order: OTOČE · BEZ · VODE · HVALA TI · ŠTO ME · NAUČI · ŽEĐATI · I · ČEZNUTI · ZA NEČIM · ČITAV SVOJ · ŽIVOT
- Full sentence (hr): *Otoče bez vode, hvala ti, što me nauči žeđati i čeznuti za nečim čitav svoj život.*
- Translation (en): *O island without water, thank you for teaching me to thirst and to long for something all my life.*
- Hint (hr): "Prva riječ je poziv otoku, a posljednja je ono što čovjek živi." / (en): "The first word calls out to the island, and the last is what a person lives."
- Then show Loda's last letter.

## Game rules for the app

- Stops can be visited in any order; each stop gives its letter once. The clue on each stop points to the next stop in one direction of the loop.
- Bonus stories (Supetar basilica and well, Kavanjin's summer house, geological column, Three Sisters, macići, Donji Humac, Olive Oil Museum, legends of Škrip, capris laudata Brattia, the viver, the pine on the roof, vitalac, Bol's plavac, the wild pear, Vičja jama) and the optional east trip (Pučišća, Selca, Povlja, Sumartin) give no letter. Show them as optional cards without a question.
- Loda's key (the Glagolitic alphabet) unlocks at the first stop the player completes, whichever it is.

## Where the letter floats out from

The letter flies out of the correct answer's text: the app finds the first character that matches the stop's letter, ignoring diacritics (C matches Č, D matches Đ). Examples: **T**oma, **Č**ile, **C**hile, Herkul (E). If the answer has no matching character (for example "a bell tower" for Z, or "vines" for L), the letter rises from the middle of the answer button instead.
