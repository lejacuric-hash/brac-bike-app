## Game 4 rules for the app

- ASTRA letters come from the five Game 3 stops; ASPERA letters from Dračevica (2nd visit), Kopačina, Selca, Sumartin, Pučišća and Škrip.
- Game 1 and 2 stops visited in Game 4 give no password letter: the player reads the letter, does the photo task and votes "true or legend".
- A stop the player already completed in an earlier game shows a new photo task instead: "Photograph a detail you missed the first time."
- Povlja gives no letter; it unlocks the frame PER ... AD ... for the finale.
